Inventory Management Dashboard: Developed an interactive Inventory Management Dashboard in Microsoft Excel to monitor inventory performance, stock availability, supplier activity, and product trends. The dashboard enables users to analyze inventory data through dynamic visualizations and interactive slicers for efficient decision-making.
Key Features:
KPI Cards displaying: Total Products, Total Stock Value, Current Stock, In Stock Items, Low Stock Items, Out of Stock Items
Monthly Purachse Trend
Supplier vs Sold Quantity Analysis
Purachse vs Sold Quantity Comparison
Category-wise Stock Value
Top 10 products
Stock Status
Slicers for Month, Suppiler, Category and Stock Status.
